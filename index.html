Fidelis Investors Undervalued Property Investment Proposal

During my internship at Fidelis Investors, a real estate private equity firm, I worked alongside an analyst to identify a potential investment opportunity for the firm. The project focused on identifying an undervalued property on Staten Island, New York, that could be added to the firm's portfolio through a Residential Transitional Loan (RTL). I first identified a potential property and analyzed its characteristics and comparable sales before proposing a loan structure. I then outlined the renovation costs and estimated the post-renovation value to evaluate the potential investment opportunity and ROI. Upon completion of the research, I presented my findings to the Managing Partners and Directors for their investment consideration.
